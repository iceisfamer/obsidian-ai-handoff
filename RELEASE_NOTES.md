# AI Handoff 0.3.0 — macOS public beta

Read a note on the left and discuss it on the right with Codex or Gemini. Ask about the full note, select a passage to attach a quote, and keep asking follow-up questions in the same conversation.

- Author: **cybrea**; license: **MIT**.
- macOS only; Obsidian **1.15.1+**; Chinese interface.
- Install and sign in to Codex or Antigravity CLI (`agy`) separately. The plugin is free; AI account, internet, and usage charges/quotas may apply.
- On Send, the note's full text, title, relative path, quote, and question go to the selected CLI/service. See the included privacy notice.
- This beta is manually installed and has not been accepted into the Community Plugins directory.
- Apple Silicon and the main Obsidian window were tested. Intel, detached windows, and third-party themes need broader testing.

Download `ai-handoff-0.3.0-macos.zip`, extract `ai-handoff` into your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/`, and enable AI Handoff. For an upgrade, preserve `data.json` and back up the existing plugin first. See README.zh-CN.md for Chinese instructions.

Tester feedback: please include macOS and Obsidian versions, client version, editor/reading mode, exact reproduction steps, and a synthetic note. Do not upload private articles, account tokens, authorization codes, or conversation databases.
